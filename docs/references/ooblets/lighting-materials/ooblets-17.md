## Transferable lesson (original work only)

In dim interiors, cluster 2-3 small emissive sources (candles, glowing clock face, bubbling liquid) at different heights to sculpt form with warm pools of light, and keep every prop silhouette rounded — even screens and vents get fat bevels.
