## Visible evidence

- Shop/club interior with glass display cases and tables presenting many distinct collectibles (hats, glasses, figurines on stands, alarm clock, lamp)
- Wall shelves with books and teapots; star-pattern wallpaper and pennant bunting
- Items individually spotlighted and generously spaced so each reads
- Multiple characters browsing; warm wood floor against purple walls

## Transferable lesson (original work only)

Display collectible unlocks on individual stands/pedestals with generous spacing and a contrasting display-surface colour — one object per pedestal makes a collection legible and makes each new unlock feel like an event.

## Do not copy

- Wallpaper pattern
- Furniture and item designs
- Character designs and neon sign glyph
