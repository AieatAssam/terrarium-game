## Visible evidence

- Dozens of blob creatures at close range with distinct silhouettes (ears, antennae, wings, cat ears, hat)
- Glossy specular highlights and soft contact shadows give the bodies volume
- Varied poses and mouth shapes (open-mouth cheer, wink) readable at gameplay distance
- Background garden is rich but creatures still separate from it

## Transferable lesson (original work only)

Give each Sprout variant one unique attachment silhouette (leaf sprout, petal collar, twig antenna) built from reusable primitive kits, and pair it with a distinct idle face so variants read at a glance even when bodies share the same blob mesh.

## Do not copy

- Slime creature designs and faces (dot eyes + open mouth)
- Specific ear/antenna/wing shapes per species
- The gardener robot character
- Trophy props and exact per-species colour pairings
