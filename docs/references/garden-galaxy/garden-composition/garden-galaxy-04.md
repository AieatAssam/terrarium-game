- Every tile edge dressed, yet the focal fountain reads instantly

## Transferable lesson (original work only)

Make water features the prestige unlock tier — a simple animated splash-ring decal plus a basin mesh gives a disproportionate charm payoff, and scatter 2-3 tiny ambient critters that hop between perch points to reward inspection.

## Do not copy

- White bird designs
- Fountain/birdbath asset shapes and balustrade/column designs
- Classical formal garden layout motif
