# Rejected Reference Candidates

Every rejected candidate, with the concrete reason it did not make the board.
Rejection does not always mean a bad screenshot — several are strong frames that
were redundant against an already-selected, stronger reference for the same
category.

## slime-rancher-2

- **slime-rancher-2-03** — Frame dominated by drone/helmet prop and industrial
  conservatory architecture; creatures are small mid/background accents.
  Redundant with stronger greenhouse frames (06, 12).
- **slime-rancher-2-04** — Decent creature + focal-device frame, but redundant:
  creature-readability is better served by 01 and scene-composition by 13;
  branded machinery dominates the transferable content.
- **slime-rancher-2-08** — Chaos set-piece: tornadoes, radial blur and haze
  swallow creature readability and habitat information.
- **slime-rancher-2-09** — First-person vac-gun fills a third of the frame;
  sparse open underwater terrain; low information for any category.
- **slime-rancher-2-10** — Good wayfinding composition, but redundant with the
  cleaner vista frame 13; branded containment-dome architecture dominates.
- **slime-rancher-2-11** — Storm set-piece: blown-out lightning core, heavy rain
  and blue-grey filter flatten materials; creatures small and obscured.

## ooblets

- **ooblets-05** — Pleasant town vista, but a civic scene rather than a garden
  or habitat frame; redundant with stronger authored-density frames (13, 14).
- **ooblets-08** — Dark single vignette around a heavily branded stickered
  machine; one small creature; no readable feedback, UI decision, or layering.
- **ooblets-10** — Small domestic cooking vignette; feedback event is mild and
  weaker than the accepted reward-feedback frames.
- **ooblets-12** — Character-driven night scene with branded costume content;
  lighting-materials is better served by tiny-glade-09 and ooblets-17, where
  surface response is inspectable.
- **ooblets-15** — Tactile vehicle construction is visible but character faces
  dominate; low confidence; tactile-geometry better served by tiny-glade-02
  and ooblets-03.
- **ooblets-16** — Redundant with ooblets-01 and ooblets-14, which teach the
  same garden-density lesson with stronger composition and closer content.
- **ooblets-18** — Heavy scanline/hologram filter and neon grid overlay make
  form and material unreadable; presentation motif is branded and
  non-transferable.

## tiny-glade

- **tiny-glade-04** — Photo-mode frame: tilt-shift blur plus exposure sliders,
  dials and toolbar; UI is camera settings, not a game decision.
- **tiny-glade-05** — Static filtered night beauty shot; buildings are blurred
  backdrop, not inspectable geometry; no gameplay-relevant information.
- **tiny-glade-06** — Pure long-distance landscape beauty shot; forms too far
  and stylised for material or tactile inspection.
- **tiny-glade-07** — Photo-filter picker: 8 near-identical thumbnails of one
  scene; redundant and unrelated to build/collection/reward decisions.
- **tiny-glade-08** — Photo-mode UI dominates; tilt-shift blur obscures most
  architecture; cityscape backdrop, not a readable personal habitat.

## garden-galaxy

- **garden-galaxy-01** — Charming and dense, but garden-composition is filled by
  the stronger garden-galaxy-04 and 06; many small branded props make it
  comparatively weaker as a clean teaching reference.
- **garden-galaxy-02** — Small floating recipe UI plus mostly bare dirt tiles
  and wide empty margins; fails ui-readability, density and composition bars.
- **garden-galaxy-05** — Too dark and atmospherically filtered for material
  inspection; repetitive forest clutter rather than an authored collection.
